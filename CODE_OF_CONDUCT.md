# Community guidelines

This project welcomes people who want to build, test, document, or simply enjoy a strange little fighting game in a terminal.

Be kind, specific, and constructive. Critique the work, not the person. Respect different experience levels, terminals, operating systems, languages, and accessibility needs. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or derail technical discussion.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the repository's security contact.
